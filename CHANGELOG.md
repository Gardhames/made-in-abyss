## [0.1.0-probe] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Initial source publication of the standalone cube/terrain/light kernel and Forge engine probe.
- Versioned island and first-layer terrain profiles, custom DH surface pipeline, and experimental GPU exact SKY implementation.
- English public documentation, issue forms, pull-request guidance, and a privacy-focused publication boundary.
- Configurable development storage and a portable client-control entry point.
- Local-only snapshots and gated milestone publication.

### Known limitations

- This is not a complete full-depth playable world or a supported player release.
- Incremental GPU block-edit integration and the full G3 regression remain unfinished.
- Acceptance fixtures, worlds, internal reports, third-party mods, and shader packs are not distributed.
